Source: [wangc90/CircCNNs](https://github.com/wangc90/CircCNNs/tree/beb68c0ff37431d5747a1a0796e8b66933cf8d1e). - **Benchmark sources:** [CircCNNs](https://github.com/wangc90/CircCNNs) and [circ2LO](https://github.com/Hhao2025/circ2LO/tree/8a74e22b1757eea3dc2e2d522ea3b8b1687d1629). Comparator implementations and weights are excluded from this repository.

- **Genomes/annotations:** references come from [UCSC](https://genome.ucsc.edu/license/), [GENCODE](https://www.gencodegenes.org/pages/data_access.html) and [RefSeq](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/). UCSC liftOver chains have separate non-commercial terms; the chain used for circBase annotation is not bundled.

Data ZIPs provide descriptors and derived prediction/figure outputs. Successful anonymous download and checksum verification establish technical integrity, not source-data redistribution permission. Third-party material is not automatically relicensed under MIT or CC0. Detailed provenance and the complete review record are retained with the manuscript/reproducibility materials.
