## Data release gate

RCM arrays are deterministic sequence descriptors aligned to the study candidate lists, not pretrained/model weights. Their generation does not itself establish rights to redistribute every source benchmark. Author approval and source-data review are required before uploading the five-dataset ZIP. If permission is unavailable, point to the original providers and retain genome-based regeneration for authorised users; identify which portions remain accessible to reviewers.

The local four-model K562 prediction archive contains genomic/gene identifiers and derived cell-line predictions, not raw participant samples. The author has uploaded it as part of the combined reproducibility package, and technical access has been verified. Applicable source-data redistribution terms still require confirmation; successful download is not proof of permission. Do not apply project MIT or a GigaDB CC0 declaration automatically to it.

Verification evidence (HTTP responses, pinned trees, license texts and checksums) is preserved in the author workspace's `submission_audit_20261023/web/`. Current local documentation changes are not yet published at the cited GitHub commit.
